# App Store Screenshot Storyboard v1.1

Status: Approved production direction

Release: Version 1.1

Primary device class: 6.9-inch iPhone, portrait

Final canvas: `1320 x 2868` PNG

Source of truth: real Version 1.1 UI captured from the iOS simulator

## 1. Story Goal

The six screenshots should tell one simple parent journey:

1. Start with a child-specific size recommendation.
2. Show that guidance is based on practical rider details.
3. Add and review a used-bike listing.
4. Understand fit, deal, risk, and the next action.
5. Save promising bikes for comparison.
6. Confirm that local data and optional AI remain under the user's control.

The sequence should feel useful and trustworthy before it feels technical. It must not imply marketplace scraping, safety certification, automatic monitoring, accounts, cloud sync, or paid features.

## 4. Composition System

### Canvas

- Export every frame at exactly `1320 x 2868` pixels.
- Use portrait orientation for the complete set.
- Keep the same background, device scale, headline position, and UI frame width across all six images.
- Use `#F5F7FA` as the main background with a restrained `#EEF5FF` brand glow or panel.
- Use brand blue `#2F6FED`, strong text `#0F172A`, and muted text `#64748B`.

### Marketing Header

- Place the headline above the app capture.
- Target two lines maximum for the headline and two lines maximum for the supporting line.
- Keep all marketing text at least `96 px` from the left and right canvas edges.
- Keep the top of text at least `120 px` from the canvas edge.
- Do not place text over the captured app UI.

### App Capture

- Use the submitted iOS build or release-candidate build in the simulator.
- Retain the real iOS status bar and the app's bottom navigation.
- Use one consistent device appearance and status-bar state.
- Do not stretch or non-uniformly scale the capture.
- Cropping is allowed only to emphasize a real section; controls and cards may not be recomposed.
- Keep at least `72 px` of visible breathing room around the app capture.
- Do not add fake taps, arrows, badges, scores, dialogs, or controls.

### Typography

- Use an installed Apple system sans-serif or the established system-font stack.
- Headline: bold, high contrast, approximately `88-104 px` on the final canvas.
- Supporting line: regular or medium, approximately `44-52 px`.
- Do not use a remote font or a decorative display font.

## 6. Production Workflow

1. Build the v1.1 release candidate with App Store mode enabled.
2. Load the approved fictional fixture without real personal or seller data.
3. Set the simulator to a supported 6.9-inch iPhone class and portrait orientation.
4. Use a clean status bar: consistent time, full signal, Wi-Fi, and battery across every capture.
5. Capture the six real app states at native simulator resolution.
6. Place captures into the shared `1320 x 2868` marketing template.
7. Apply only background, headline, supporting line, placement, and uniform scaling.
8. Export PNG in sRGB with no transparency.
9. Verify exact pixel dimensions and visually inspect at 100%.
10. Compare every visible feature and claim against the release-candidate build.

Browser captures may be used for layout drafts, but final App Store files must come from the iOS simulator or the submitted native build.

## 7. Review Checklist

- Six frames form one coherent journey in the approved order.
- Headline and supporting line match this document exactly.
- All visible UI exists in the v1.1 release candidate.
- Result labels and statuses are generated by the app, not manually edited.
- No private, real, or marketplace-derived seller data appears.
- No account, payment, subscription, scraping, monitoring, or cloud-sync claim appears.
- No screenshot implies safety certification or guaranteed fit.
- No frame relies on color alone to communicate status.
- Status bar, device frame, margins, and type scale are consistent.
- Bottom navigation is visible and the active tab matches the frame.
- Text remains readable in App Store search results and product-page previews.
- Each export is exactly `1320 x 2868`, portrait, PNG, and sRGB.
- If iPad remains enabled, create a separate iPad composition from real iPad captures.

## 8. Apple Submission Reference

Apple currently allows one to ten screenshots in PNG or JPEG formats. For the 6.9-inch iPhone class, accepted portrait sizes include:

- `1260 x 2736`
- `1290 x 2796`
- `1320 x 2868`

This storyboard standardizes the v1.1 set on `1320 x 2868`. Recheck the official specification immediately before App Store Connect upload:

https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications/
