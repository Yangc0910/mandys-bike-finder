### Description

Mandy's Bike Finder helps parents make faster, clearer decisions when browsing used kids bike listings.

Create a child profile with height, age, and riding experience. Then evaluate one bike listing at a time using a screenshot, pasted link or text, or manual entry. The app provides practical guidance on likely fit, value, risk, and next steps.

Key features:

- Child bike-fit profile stored on your device.
- Listing evaluation from a screenshot, pasted link or text, or manual details.
- Optional, user-triggered AI screenshot extraction.
- Local fallback analysis for fit, value, and risk.
- A clear recommendation and practical next steps.
- Seller message draft with useful questions.
- Saved evaluations and shortlist history on your device.
- Privacy information and local-data controls in Settings.

No account or subscription is required. The app does not automatically scrape marketplace pages. AI screenshot extraction only begins after you explicitly request it.

Mandy's Bike Finder provides decision support only. Always inspect bike fit, brakes, tires, frame condition, and ride comfort before purchase.

## App Review Information

### Review Notes

Mandy's Bike Finder is a decision-support app for parents evaluating used kids bike listings.

- No account, payment, subscription, or in-app purchase is required.
- Child profiles and saved evaluations are stored locally on the device.
- The app does not automatically scrape Facebook, OfferUp, Craigslist, or login-gated marketplace pages.
- Links, pasted text, and screenshots are user-provided references.
- Selecting a screenshot creates a local preview only.
- AI extraction starts only after the user taps `Extract details with AI`.
- Initial app load does not call OpenAI or another LLM.
- Manual entry and local fallback analysis work without AI.
- Provider credentials remain server-side.
- Settings lets users clear the child profile, history, or all local app data.

Suggested review path:

1. Open Profile and create or edit a child profile.
2. Open Evaluate and choose Manual entry.
3. Enter a title, price, and wheel size.
4. Review the listing and run the evaluation.
5. Save the result to History.
6. Open Settings to review privacy information and local-data controls.

No special login credentials are required.
